## What this will cover

- What an early-arriving fact is: a fact that lands **before** its dimension member exists
- Why it breaks joins (and why it's really "the dimension is late relative to the fact")
- The standard fix: **inferred (placeholder) members** — insert a stub dimension row with an `is_inferred` flag
- Catch-up: when the real dimension row arrives, Type 1-update the stub and clear the flag
- Demo: an order arrives for customer 999 — one day before customer 999's dimension record exists
